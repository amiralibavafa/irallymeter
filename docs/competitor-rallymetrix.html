iRallyMeter vs RallyMetrix

Competitive analysis for Amirali · 14 August 2026 · prepared by Saam
Their app downloaded, installed and run. Analysis from public materials, the installed package, and the app itself.

A correction to something said earlier. An initial read of their website suggested their app required Android 8 while ours ran on Android 6, which would have been a real advantage to us. Both numbers were wrong. Their installed package declares a minimum of Android 7.0, ours declares Android 7.0, and they are identical. Their own marketing page overstates their requirement. Our Android 6 claim was never achievable, because a library the app depends on does not support it. Neither product has an advantage here.

2. The finding that matters most

Their app opens with a mandatory phone-number login and an SMS verification code. There is no way past it. Using the app at all requires a working mobile number, a network connection to receive the code, and their server being reachable.

Their website states the app offers "completely offline operation" and works in areas "without mobile network coverage". Their own first screen contradicts that. It may well run offline once signed in, but it cannot be reached offline.

For a rally computer this is a meaningful weakness, and it is worst in exactly the situation the product exists for. At a remote start area, on a phone with no signal, on a fresh install, their app is a login form and ours is a trip computer. Their server is also a single point of failure for every user at once, and a subscription that lapses mid-event is an account problem rather than a measurement problem.

iRallyMeter has no account, no server and no login. It measures from first launch with no network, permanently.

3. What they claim, and what is missing from it

Their marketing lists a satellite speedometer, Trip 1 and Trip 2, real-time navigation, an "intelligent rally telemetry system", GPS and GLONASS, and fully offline operation.

Tunnels are never mentioned once. Their own description of how the app works is "speed, acceleration and distance calculated directly from the GPS hardware chipset". That is an accurate description of an app that stops measuring when the sky disappears.

Estimating distance through a blackout from motion sensors, then reconciling against GPS on recovery, is the bulk of what this revision of iRallyMeter built. In a market whose cities contain Niayesh and Alborz, and whose rallies run mountain roads, that is not a minor feature.

They also appear to have no background measurement

The permissions their app requests are internet, network state, fine and coarse location, and wake lock. It does not request a foreground service, a location foreground service, or notifications. Without those, Android throttles or stops location updates when the screen turns off or the app moves to the background.

iRallyMeter runs an Android foreground service specifically so the trip counter keeps measuring with the screen off, which is the normal state of a phone on a windscreen mount during a long transit.

This one is inferred from the permission list rather than observed. It is strong evidence, not proof.

4. Where they are genuinely ahead

  • They shipped. Three builds, a working download, a free trial and a subscription. iRallyMeter is an unmerged branch with no release channel, no listing and no pricing.
  • Persian. They have an in-app English and Persian toggle. iRallyMeter is English only, and the open question about Persian-Indic digits has not been answered.
  • Real-time navigation. iRallyMeter has a map, not navigation.
  • A business model exists. Ours does not.

5. Honest limits of this analysis

  • Only the login screen was seen. Going further needed a phone number and their SMS code, and working around another product's authentication is not something worth doing.
  • Their tunnel handling, navigation quality and telemetry could all be better than their marketing suggests. Absence from a website is not absence from the product.
  • The background-measurement gap is inferred from permissions.

The test that would settle it is a drive. Run both apps on the same phone over the same road, through a tunnel, and compare the distance each reports against a known figure. That single measurement is worth more than any amount of further teardown, and it is something your father is already positioned to do.

6. Recommendation

Do not chase their feature list. Navigation and telemetry are where they are ahead, and both are large builds that move iRallyMeter away from what it is good at.

Press the two places they are structurally weak, because neither is a quick fix for them. A login wall is an architectural decision tied to their subscription, and measuring through a blackout is most of a product. Those are the two sentences worth putting in front of a crew:

  • It keeps measuring inside a tunnel.
  • It works with no account, no signal and no subscription.

Then close the gaps that are cheap. Persian is a translation pass, not an engineering problem, and it is the one place they are ahead that costs us little to match.
