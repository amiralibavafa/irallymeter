iRallyMeter — what we changed

Saam & Claude · audit against SPEC-v2 · 4 August 2026 · for Amirali

Short version. Your architecture was right. The distance engine was already pure Dart, already single-integration speed-hold, already had automatic tunnel detection, and there was no Firebase to rip out. What we found were specific bugs inside a good design, plus five places where the code predated Revision 2 of the spec.

Nothing is merged. Everything is on two branches, SA-V1 and SA-V2. Your main has not been touched once.

The bugs that actually mattered

Each one below is real, was reproduced, and has a number attached. They are in order of how much damage they did.

1. A faster GPS made the app measure less

The noise-gating rule threw away any movement smaller than the fix's own accuracy — but it also moved the reference point when it did that, so the movement was destroyed instead of ignored. The cutoff works out to accuracy × fixRate, which means that at the 5 Hz the app itself asks for, everything below 90 km/h was being discarded.

It hid for fourteen steps because every test fixture ran at 1 Hz and highway speed. Fix: hold the reference point when the floor rejects a sample. Stop-and-go traffic went from −5.04 % to −0.12 %, and a parked car still accumulates exactly zero.

2. Estimation Mode could get stuck on, and invented distance while stuck

The app measures with any fix up to 25 m accuracy. But §15.2 only lets it leave Estimation Mode on a fix of 20 m or better, and nothing else ended the mode. So fixes between 21 m and 25 m were a trap: good enough to measure with, not good enough to escape with. Light tree cover sits in exactly that band.

The damage is not noise. A dead-reckoning estimate holds the speed you entered at, so it cannot see the car slowing down. In the test: the car halved its speed and the app reported 2400 m for 1200 m of real travel. That error is permanent, because the correction logic only ever adds distance back, never removes it.

Fix: a sustained run of usable, consistent fixes now also ends the mode. §15.2 is untouched — three good fixes still exit immediately.

3. A tunnel is silence, and the first fix made it worse

We found the location stream could never recover if you switched location services off and back on. The first attempt at a fix was a plain timeout, and it was worse than the bug: on the phone it restarted the Android foreground service about twenty times inside one 400-second tunnel — the exact service keeping the app alive in there.

Rebuilt so tearing the stream down needs evidence, not just quiet. Measured at zero restarts across 150 s and 90 s of silence.

4. iOS had never actually been configured

_settings() returned AndroidSettings on every platform. It compiled and ran because AndroidSettings is a LocationSettings, so the failure was completely silent. iOS was getting default accuracy, no activity type, and pauseLocationUpdatesAutomatically left at its default — which pauses location whenever iOS decides the car has stopped. That is every start line and every time control.

5. Resetting a trip leaked the previous leg into the new one

Up to 715 m of the old leg's tunnel correction was still being paid out into the new leg. On a rally that is the difference between “turn after 3 km” landing on the right junction and the wrong one.

6. The compass — three separate causes

You said it was laggy and inaccurate. It was all three of these at once:

  • the smoothing used a per-sample weight, so the lag was a property of the handset's sensor rate rather than of time
  • “Use true north” only changed a label. No declination was ever applied, so it showed a magnetic reading and called it true
  • tilt compensation worked out “which way is down” from the raw accelerometer, so it followed braking and cornering — wrong exactly when you are looking at it

That same per-sample-filter mistake turned up three times in the codebase: speed display, compass needle, compass tilt. Worth watching for.

7. The app could fail to start at all

main() waited on two permission requests before runApp() with no error handling. On a first install they raced, threw, and the app hung on the Flutter logo forever.

What else changed

  • Manual TUNNEL buttons deleted (SPEC-v2 §14/§15) — detection is fully automatic now, with a section log you can read in Settings.
  • Speed comes from GNSS Doppler, with position differentiation only as a fallback when Doppler is untrustworthy.
  • Estimated state is visible — amber digits plus an EST / EST? / SYNC badge, on all four readouts. The badge is text, not just colour, because at night the whole palette is one hue family and colour alone tells you nothing.
  • Portrait no longer overflows. Your own skipped test recorded 142 px, and it grew with the status text — 137 px in Estimation Mode. The nav icons now sit on their own row in portrait only. Nothing was shrunk, and the touch targets ended up further apart.
  • Permissions are explained before they are asked for, which both stores require and which was a rejection risk.
  • Removed ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION — it was declared in the manifest and never once requested, so it was never granted and did nothing except add store-review burden.
  • A record-and-replay harness with nine synthetic drives, so accuracy claims are measured against known ground truth instead of asserted.

What is still wrong — please read this part

These three are not fixed, and none of them can be fixed from a desk.

  1. Urban canyon under-reads by 12.50 % against the spec's 1 % target. Its test is skipped but written at full strength — deliberately never weakened. We did not tune this, because tuning against a synthetic fixture with no real-world number to check against is guessing, and the same threshold is what keeps a parked car at exactly 0.000 km.
  2. No road test has ever happened. Every accuracy figure in this document comes from generated fixtures where the true answer is exact by construction, or from an emulator. A better replay is not a drive.
  3. The app has never run on iOS. It compiles for the simulator. That is the entire extent of it.

The road test — the part only a car can do

Full protocol in docs/ROAD-TEST.md. Four things, in priority order. Open Settings → Section log for the GNSS HEALTH panel and screenshot it.

  1. Mid-drive, switch location services off for a minute, then back on. Do not restart the app. The trip counter must start advancing again. This is the one fix whose code path is certain but whose on-device behaviour we could not reproduce either way on an emulator. If this fails, Phase 3 reopens — it is not a small ticket.
  2. A real tunnel (Niayesh or Alborz). Distance within about 3 %, no jump or reversal on exit, and STREAM STALLS must still read 0.
  3. 50 km against measured markers. The target is 1 %.
  4. City traffic and a twisty road. These do not have to pass. They have to be measured, because those two numbers are what decide whether we touch the noise-gating threshold at all.

Numbers, not impressions. If something fails, send the number and a screenshot of the section log. The two that are stop-ship are a trip counter that freezes and does not recover, and any visible jump or reversal when you come out of a tunnel.
